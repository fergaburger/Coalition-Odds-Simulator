Coalition Odds Simulator

Enter each party's polling share, coalition alignment, and number of electorates it wins. The simulator estimates how often the left or right reaches a parliamentary majority.

Model: New Zealand MMP Simulations: 50,000 Responses per poll: 832 Threshold: 5% or an electorate seat

Enter each party's electorate seat count. Electorate seats are guaranteed; if they exceed a party's vote-based allocation, only the extra seats are added to Parliament.

Selected party stays at its entered share in every simulated poll.

Use one row per party (do not combine several sub-5% parties into an "Other" row). If shares total less than 100%, the remainder is allocated proportionally across parties with entered poll share; when a party is held fixed, the remainder is shared among the other parties. Each poll samples 832 responses; a party qualifies for seats when its simulated vote is at least 5% or it wins at least one electorate. Seats are allocated among qualifying parties using the Sainte-Laguë method. A party that wins electorates receives at least that many seats; any seats above its party-vote allocation create an overhang and increase Parliament's size. Majority thresholds adjust to the resulting seat count. The approximate 95% polling margin at a 50% share is ±3.4 percentage points. This is a simplified polling model, not an official election forecast.
